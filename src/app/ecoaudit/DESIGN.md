# EcoAudit design

EcoAudit is an independent project brief for hackathon judges. The opening explains what the tool does, names the three inputs, and puts the demo and project links within reach.

- **Palette:** Off-white (#FAFAF8), charcoal (#171717), secondary gray (#525252), and quiet dividers (#DEDED9). The original EcoAudit mark retains its colors.
- **Typography:** Newsreader at 48px and 600 weight for the desktop headline. The small-screen headline scales from 34px to 40px. Geist is used throughout the rest of the page.
- **Layout:** At desktop widths, two rows pair overview/demo and research/team in a 1200px container with a 64px column gap. At 1100px and below, sections stack in that order.
- **Controls:** Project actions use thin neutral outlines. Full screen sits beside the demo heading. Run locally, Résumé and Portfolio use underlined text, while contact icons have accessible labels and 44px targets.
- **Attribution:** Research links pair source marks and brand-colored labels with bold titles. The shared footer credits Campus to Cloud Hackathon, presented by Techfest, IIT Bombay and sponsored by AWS, and links to the MIT License.

Design process reference: [Unslop by Damir Sokolovsky](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop), CC BY 4.0.
